# Third-party notices

Source: skills from a local Hermes Agent installation, including active and optional skills. Upstream project: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent . Some locally authored skills may not exist in that upstream project.

Original authors, frontmatter metadata and bundled license/notice files are retained where available. Pi compatibility edits are additions to the original material, not a relicensing of it.

Declared frontmatter licenses for the 217 included skills:

- MIT: 195 skills.
- Apache-2.0: 9 skills.
- Unspecified: 13 skills; ownership and redistribution permission need review.

Verified upstream MIT declarations were restored for `heartmula` and `polymarket`. Their original instruction bodies match the tracked Hermes versions; only frontmatter differed. The MIT notice is preserved in `licenses/Hermes-MIT.txt`. See `LICENSE_REVIEW.md` for the remaining unresolved skills.

`catalog.json` identifies each skill's declared license. A frontmatter declaration does not verify the licensing of every bundled third-party asset. Before public distribution, check upstream copyright notices, applicable license texts, bundled assets, and permission for the unspecified skills. Do not assume the Hermes repository license automatically covers locally authored skills.

No blanket license or redistribution clearance is asserted by this package.
